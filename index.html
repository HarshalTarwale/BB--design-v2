Our story

Why one team does all of it.

Backkbenchers started with a simple problem: brands were paying one agency for Instagram, another for hoardings, and a third to shoot the photos both of them needed. Nobody was talking to anybody.

One team. One strategy. One number to call.

Today we run socials, performance, SEO and websites alongside metro pillars, billboards and broadcast — and we shoot, edit and animate everything ourselves. Whatever shape the brief arrives in, you get clarity back.
